Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Customer CRM automation project handles personal data when providing customer registration, birthday reminders, promotional vouchers and related messaging workflows for participating businesses, including restaurants and salons.

This project is currently being prepared for testing. Business-specific privacy notices and contractual responsibilities must be confirmed before onboarding real customers.

Who is responsible

For inquiries about the Customer CRM project, contact abdulaziz2463@gmail.com. Participating restaurants or salons may be responsible for determining why and how their own customer data is used; where we process information on their behalf, their privacy notice and instructions also apply.

Information processed

Depending on a business's registration form and configuration, records can include customer name, phone number, birthday or birth month, consent and opt-out status, voucher code, eligibility, redemption status, message history and delivery status.

Purposes

Information is used to register customers, determine birthday-offer eligibility, issue and validate vouchers, send opted-in WhatsApp messages, record redemption, handle opt-outs, provide support and prevent duplicate messages or misuse.

Systems and providers

The project uses Google Forms and Google Sheets for registration and records, self-hosted n8n to run automations, and the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform for messaging. These providers may process data under their own terms, privacy notices and regional data-processing arrangements.

Marketing choices

Promotional WhatsApp messages should be sent only where the relevant business has obtained an appropriate opt-in. Recipients can opt out by replying STOP or contacting the business that collected their details. Opt-out requests should be reflected in the sending records before future promotions.

Retention and security

Records are retained only as needed for the purposes described, operational recordkeeping and applicable legal requirements. Retention periods and deletion procedures must be set with each participating business. Access should be restricted to authorized operators and system credentials must be protected. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your requests

To ask about access, correction, deletion or marketing opt-out, contact the business that collected your information or email abdulaziz2463@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request and coordinate with the relevant business before acting. Legal rights vary by location.

International processing and changes

Services used in the workflow may involve processing across borders. Applicable safeguards and disclosures must be reviewed for each launch market. This notice may change as the product and operations evolve; updated versions will appear on this page.
