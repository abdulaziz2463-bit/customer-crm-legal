Data Deletion Instructions

If your information was registered with a restaurant, salon or other business using Customer CRM, you can request its deletion or correction.

How to request deletion

  1. Contact the business where you registered, or email abdulaziz2463@gmail.com with the subject Customer CRM data deletion request.
  2. Identify the business involved and the phone number used for registration. Do not send passwords, access tokens, government identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information.
  3. We will review the request, verify authority where necessary, and coordinate with the business responsible for the customer record.

What may be removed

As applicable, records can include registration details, birthday information, voucher history and messaging-related fields in Google Sheets or related workflow stores. Some data may need to be retained for legal compliance, dispute handling, security or transactional records; we will explain where applicable.

Stopping messages

To stop promotional messages promptly, reply STOP to the business's WhatsApp message or contact that business. An opt-out can be recorded without requiring a full deletion request.

Third-party records

Information retained independently by Meta, Google or other providers is governed by their policies. Requests involving their platforms may need to be made separately.
